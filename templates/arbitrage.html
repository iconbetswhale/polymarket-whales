THE MATH

How IconLabs sizes an arbitrage

01

Convert every price

American odds become decimal odds. Any exchange commission buffer is applied only to net winnings.

02

Confirm the edge

Add the inverse decimal odds across every mutually exclusive outcome. A total below 1.00 is an arbitrage.

return % = (1 / Σ(1 ÷ decimal odds) − 1) × 100
03

Equalize the payout

Allocate stake in proportion to each outcome's inverse odds, then balance cents to maximize the lowest payout.

stakeᵢ = total stake × (1 ÷ oddsᵢ) / Σ(1 ÷ odds)
Structural safeguards

IconLabs requires a complete matching outcome set, the exact same line and participant, fresh quotes, valid prices, and a positive return after configured fees and cent rounding.
