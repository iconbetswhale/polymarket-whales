How IconLabs sizes a middle

Find a real gap

For totals, a lower Over is paired with a higher Under. For spreads, opposing lines must overlap. The bets must describe the same event and market.

Equalize outside payouts

The bet is split in inverse proportion to effective decimal odds, then rounded to cents. That maximizes the smaller payout when the result lands outside the middle.

Price the lottery ticket honestly

Cost is the worst outside loss divided by total bet. Break-even is cost divided by the difference between middle profit and worst outside profit. No unverified hit probability is assumed.
