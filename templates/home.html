Frequently Asked Questions

How does IconLabs work?

IconLabs combines tracked sharp-wallet activity, market pricing, line movement, and model rules to surface qualified opportunities with transparent confidence and sizing context.

How many exchanges do I need?

You can begin with one supported exchange, but access to several books or exchanges gives you more chances to secure the best executable price.

How long does this take each day?

The dashboard does the monitoring continuously. Most traders only need a few minutes to review a signal, compare prices, and record their entry.

Will every tracked play win?

No. Variance is unavoidable, and no model wins every position. IconLabs is designed to improve decision quality, pricing discipline, and long-run expected value.

How can I understand why a play qualified?

Every qualified play includes its supporting wallets, conflicts, confidence inputs, executable pricing, recommended sizing, and later closing-line measurements.

What if I want to cancel?

You can manage or cancel your plan from your account. Your access remains active through the end of the current billing period.
