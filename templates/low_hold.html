How IconLabs calculates Low Hold

01

Convert each price

American odds become decimal odds and implied probabilities. Exchange fees reduce net winnings, never the returned bet amount.

02

Measure the hold

Add the implied probabilities of every opposing leg and subtract 100%.

hold % = (Σ(1 ÷ decimal odds) − 1) × 100
03

Equalize outside outcomes

By default, keep the baseline amount fixed and solve each hedge from its target payout. Total bankroll mode remains available.

hedgeᵢ = (baseline × odds₁) ÷ oddsᵢ
04

Model the middle

For compatible Over/Under lines, IconLabs enumerates attainable integer results between the lines and shows whether both bets win or one wins while the other pushes.

Transparent and independently implemented

The formulas are standard sportsbook hold and hedge math. IconLabs does not claim access to another product's proprietary ranking, source weights, or market-matching code.
